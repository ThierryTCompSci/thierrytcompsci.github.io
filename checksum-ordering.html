    Need a hint?

    Think about who acts first. The sender must calculate something before transmitting it. The receiver then uses the received data to calculate a value for comparison. Keep both possible outcomes together in the final step.

    Remember

    A checksum detects errors; it does not fix the data. Matching checksums mean that no error was detected, but do not guarantee that the data is unchanged. Different data can sometimes produce the same checksum.
