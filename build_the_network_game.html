Build the Network

You are the network engineer. Place the correct devices into the network, then test whether a packet can travel successfully from a school computer to the internet.

Mission

Drag a device to a numbered position in the diagram. You can also click a device and then click a position. Click a placed device to return it to the device bank. Use the connections and device roles you have learned to decide where each device belongs. The numbered positions do not give you function hints.
