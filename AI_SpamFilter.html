    How to use this page

    Work through the missions in any order. Explore the model where one is provided, answer the questions, then press Check answer. Feedback explains the reasoning. You can retry as often as needed, use a hint or inspect the model answer. For ordering tasks, use the arrow buttons, keyboard focus or drag cards; dragging is optional.

    Progress is saved only in this browser profile and at this page address. Clearing browser data, using another computer or changing browser can remove it. Successful answers are saved separately from later drafts. This page works offline, with all CSS, JavaScript and diagrams embedded. No personal data or answers are uploaded. Simulations are simplified teaching models and do not access real devices.
