Sort the workload

Place all six task cards in the CPU or GPU container, then press Check my answers. You can move cards between containers or return a selected card to the tray.

On a touchscreen, tap a card and then its destination container. With a keyboard, use Tab to focus a card or container button, and Enter or Space to activate it. Escape cancels a selection.

For the graphics, machine-learning and simulation tasks, assume suitable software and workloads that make use of GPU parallel processing. CPUs and GPUs often work together.
