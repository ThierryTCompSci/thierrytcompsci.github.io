Make the connections

Choose a component on the left, then its definition on the right. You can also drag between the circular connection points, starting from either column. Match all 13 pairs.

Press Check my connections when ready. Correct pairs turn green and lock. Incorrect pairs stay editable. Select a connected component to change its destination, or click an unsecured line to remove it.

Keyboard: Tab to a card; Enter or Space selects it. Arrow keys move between cards and columns. Delete removes an unsecured connection; Escape cancels a selection.
