Controls, scoring and difficulty

Decode: use the eight place values to form the highlighted number. Click a bit or use the physical number-row keys 1–8, left to right. Space fires, Backspace clears, P pauses and F activates a charged freeze. With keyboard focus on a bit, Enter toggles it. You can click a falling signal to target it.

  • Three modes: Practice has no falling deadline. Arcade begins with 0–15 and expands to the full 0–255 range. Challenge uses the full range from the start and adds more simultaneous signals.
  • Six sectors: advance after every five correct conversions. New signals become gradually faster; signals already on screen keep their own pace.
  • Combos: every three consecutive correct shots raises the multiplier, up to ×4. Quick rescues earn a time bonus; gold signals earn an extra bonus.
  • Freeze: five correct conversions charge a four-second freeze. Activate it when you need thinking time. It pauses falling and new arrivals while you keep decoding.
  • Three shields: a missed signal costs one shield. A wrong shot resets the streak but lets you correct the bits. After a missed signal, new signals receive extra thinking time; clean streaks gradually remove that assistance.

Effects: optional particles, background motion and sound can be switched off. Falling is the game’s core motion. The game automatically pauses when its tab is hidden or its window loses focus. Progress is kept during this run; only personal-best scores are saved.

8-bit unsigned binary: 0000 0000–1111 1111 represents denary 0–255. One independent HTML file. Works offline.
