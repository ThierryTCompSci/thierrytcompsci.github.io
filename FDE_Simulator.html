Sample challenge: 7 + 5

0: LDA 16 → 1: ADD 17 → 2: STA 18 → 3: HLT
Data: RAM[16] = 7 and RAM[17] = 5.
Predict the result. Step through the program and look for the answer in ACC and RAM[18].

01 / TWO ADDRESSESWhere to store vs. where to look

Storing LDA 16 at address 0 means the instruction lives at RAM[0]. Its operand, 16, tells the CPU to read the data at RAM[16].

02 / INSTRUCTIONS AND DATAA number is not an instruction

Use “A data value” to place a number such as 7 in RAM. Use “An instruction” to tell the CPU what to do with a number. A program usually begins at address 0.

03 / CONTROL FLOWStop or choose the next instruction

The PC advances during fetch. JMP changes it to another address. CMP sets ZF; JZ jumps when ZF is 1 and JNZ when it is 0. HLT stops the program.

About this simplified simulator

This is an assembly-style teaching model, not a particular processor. LDA, STA, ADD, SUB and CMP use direct addressing: their operand is a RAM address. Jump operands are instruction addresses. Data can be positive or negative whole numbers within JavaScript’s safe integer range. CMP alone updates the comparison flag here. Four clicks show two fetch actions, then decode and execute. Real hardware may use more micro-operations; a data access during execute is grouped into that step. MDR shows memory transfers; IR retains the current instruction.

Works locally in your browser. Your program is not uploaded or saved between page reloads.
