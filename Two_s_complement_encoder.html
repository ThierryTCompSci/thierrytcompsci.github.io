1.1 Number systems

Before you start

An 8-bit two’s complement number has place values −128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1, giving the range −128 to 127. For a negative number: write its absolute value (distance to zero) in 8-bit binary, flip every bit, then add 1 in binary. For zero or a positive number, use ordinary binary without flipping or adding 1.
