Step 2: What might affect you?

Read each risk and tick the ones that could happen to you. You do not need to share whether something has already happened.

There are no wrong answers. If you are unsure, you can tick a risk to learn how to stay safer. Many apps let you limit who can contact you in Privacy or Safety settings, but the options differ by app.

Step 3: My Personal Online Safety Improvement Plan

Follow each small step. Tick it only after you have actually done it. Menu names may differ between apps.

You can print your plan or use your browser's Save as PDF option. Your choices stay in this browser and are not sent to a server.

⭐ Safety rules for everyone

This is a learning activity, not a safety test. If someone online makes you feel unsafe, stop replying and speak to a trusted adult. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. No information is uploaded or stored by this page.
