About original colour depth and this simulation

Browsers decode images to RGB plus transparency. The experiment uses up to 24 RGB bits per pixel and composites transparency onto white. Source depth is read from PNG, GIF, BMP and supported JPEG headers when available; other formats are labelled as unknown, rather than guessed. A source above 24 bits is demonstrated at the browser’s 24-bit RGB precision. Indexed source depths describe palette indices. Reduced-depth palettes are deliberately fixed, not optimised for each image. Animated files use a single decoded frame. The displayed estimate is not the uploaded file size.

Made for curiosity. Images are processed locally in your browser.
