About these activities

14 activity pages, 103 missions. Based on the supplied Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science 0478 syllabus, section 3.2 (2026–2028), and supplied textbook pages 88–117. The activities and schematic illustrations are original teaching material. Mechanism simulations are simplified models; they do not access real devices. Every page includes its own CSS and JavaScript. Keep these files together to use the collection links.

Browser storage depends on the profile and page address. Keep using the same location to retain progress. If opening local files has restricted storage in your browser, use a consistent school website address.

Learn by trying, checking and explaining.
